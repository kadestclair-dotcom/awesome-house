# Supporting Awesome House

Awesome House is free, and every feature is free for everyone. Support is a thank-you, never a key.

## Why nothing is locked

The app is built on Blizzard's Developer API. Its [terms](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/a2989b50-5f16-43b1-abec-2ae17cc09dd6/blizzard-developer-api-terms-of-use) (section 2.02) say apps may not offer "premium" versions with extra paid features, may not charge players to download the app or for services related to it, and may not show pop-ups asking for donations. So there is no paid tier, no early access, no password-protected feature, and no donation nag inside the app.

## Ways to support

- **PayPal:** [paypal.me/keedoyesyes](https://paypal.me/keedoyesyes)
- Star this repository, report bugs, and tell other players about it.

## What supporters get

Only things that live outside the app:

- Your name (or character name) in a **Supporters** list in the README
- A supporter role and a supporters-only chat channel on Discord, if there is one
- Behind-the-scenes posts and devlogs about what's being built
- A vote on what gets worked on next

Supporters never get app features, early downloads or faster data. Everyone gets the same app on the same day.

### Notes for Kade: setting up Patreon or Ko-fi later

If you open a Patreon or Ko-fi page, keep the tiers to the perks above. Avoid:

- **Early access to new versions or beta builds.** That's paying to get features that use Blizzard's API.
- **Supporter-only downloads or GitHub Sponsors "sponsor-only" repositories.** That's charging to download.
- **Unlock codes, passwords or license keys** for any part of the app.
- **Splash screens or pop-ups in the app asking for money.** The Help menu link and Support button (no pop-up) are fine.

A tier sketch that stays inside those lines:

| Tier | Price | Perks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper | $1/mo | Name in the README Supporters list |
| Silver | $3/mo | + Discord supporter role and channel, devlogs |
| Gold | $5/mo | + Vote on the roadmap each month |

Once the page exists, add its link next to PayPal here, in `README.md`, and in the app's Help menu (`DONATE_URL` near the top of `wow_price_gui.pyw` can stay PayPal or point to the new page).
